# LinkedIn template

To validate by the owner before the first send, and to adapt at setup: replace `<the problem in plain words>` with the project's problem, in words the target uses. In the first message only one variable changes from one person to the next: `{personal line}`, a true, sourced sentence from the research (Contacts, "Personal line").

Connection note: 300 characters max, counted before proposing. If the person accepts without answering, one follow-up only, 5 working days later, carrying a second variable `{question}`: a precise question about their work, from their personal line, their signal or what we learned in calls (nobody named). Written at follow-up time, per [`writing.md`](writing.md) rule 7, 200 characters max.

## French

**Demande de connexion (personne qui vit le problème)**
> Bonjour {prénom}, {personal line} Chez Hexa (le studio d'Aircall et Spendesk), j'étudie <the problem in plain words>. Auriez-vous 20 min pour me raconter la dernière fois que c'est arrivé chez vous ? J'apprends, je ne vends rien.

**Demande de connexion (expert)**
> Bonjour {prénom}, {personal line} Chez Hexa (le studio d'Aircall et Spendesk), je cherche à comprendre <the problem in plain words>. Auriez-vous 20 min pour m'en parler ? J'apprends, je ne vends rien.

**Relance (une seule)**
> Merci pour la connexion, {prénom}. Une question plus précise, si ça peut aider : {question}. 20 minutes suffisent, quand ça vous arrange.

## English

**Connection request (someone who has the problem)**
> Hi {first name}, {personal line} At Hexa (the studio behind Aircall and Spendesk), I'm looking at <the problem in plain words>. Would you have 20 min to tell me about the last time it happened on your side? I'm learning, not selling.

**Connection request (expert)**
> Hi {first name}, {personal line} At Hexa (the studio behind Aircall and Spendesk), I'm trying to understand <the problem in plain words>. Would you have 20 min to talk about it? I'm learning, not selling.

**Follow-up (only one)**
> Thanks for connecting, {first name}. A more specific question, if it helps: {question}. 20 minutes is plenty, whenever suits you.

## First-degree connections (from the `network` skill)

No note and no 300-character limit: a short, direct message in the tone of a connection, same structure (personal line, the problem, one ask), plus an intro ask when they are a door to a target account.

## Personal line examples
- "I saw you are building the group procurement team."
- "I read your post on moving from prototype to series."
- "I saw your talk at <event> on <topic>."
