# LinkedIn template

The pattern and the rules are in [`writing.md`](writing.md). Variables that change from one person to the next:
- `{credibility}`: an intro if there is one, else the Hexa anchor closest to their field;
- `{topic}`: the discovery's subject, in their words;
- `{hook}`: the CRM `Personal line` (intro, experience or dated signal), optional;
- `{slot}`: "aujourd'hui", "ce jeudi", "cette semaine".

A connection note is 300 characters max: count before proposing.

## Our anchors (filled at setup with the owner)

| Their field | Anchor sentence |
| --- | --- |
| default | « le startup studio derrière Aircall et Spendesk » / "the startup studio behind Aircall and Spendesk" |
| <field> | « le startup studio derrière <Hexa company> (<what it does in 3 words>) » |

## French

**With an intro**
> Hello {prénom},
>
> Je bosse avec {nom} chez Hexa, il m'a conseillé de t'écrire.
>
> On cherche à créer notre prochaine startup dans {domaine}, notamment sur {sujet}. J'aurais quelques questions à te poser.
>
> Tu aurais 20 min {créneau} ?

**Without an intro (Hexa anchor)**
> Hello {prénom},
>
> Je suis chez Hexa, {ancre}. On veut lancer notre prochaine startup {domaine}, sur {sujet}.
>
> J'ai quelques questions pour toi {accroche}. Ça te dirait d'en parler 20 min {créneau} ? ;)

**Follow-up (only one, 5 working days later)**
> Hello {prénom}, une question plus précise, si ça peut aider : {question}. 15 min suffisent, quand tu veux cette semaine.

## English

**With an intro**
> Hi {first name},
>
> I work with {name} at Hexa, who suggested I reach out.
>
> We're starting our next company in {domain}, around {topic}, and I'd love to ask you a few questions.
>
> Would you have 20 min {slot}?

**Without an intro (Hexa anchor)**
> Hi {first name},
>
> I'm at Hexa, {anchor}. We're starting our next {domain} company, on {topic}.
>
> I have a few questions for you {hook}. Up for a 20-min chat {slot}?

**Follow-up (only one)**
> Hi {first name}, a more specific question, if it helps: {question}. 15 min is plenty, any time this week.

## First-degree connections (from the `network` skill)

Same pattern, no 300-character limit, in the tone of a connection. If they are a door to a target account, add a named intro ask.
