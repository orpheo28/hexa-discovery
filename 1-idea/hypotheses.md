# The hypotheses

The idea in force is in [`current.md`](current.md). Each hypothesis is mirrored to the shared Notion "Discovery – Hypotheses" (same code, status and evidence).

**How to read the evidence.**
- "evidence": a call with someone who has the problem or pays, with an exact quote and timestamp.
- "expert opinion": an expert or investor call, counted apart.
- "field listening": a third-party source (video, article, study). It never changes a status alone (rule 8).

Statuses: To test, Testing, Validated, Refuted (and "archived" here when merged into another or tied to an abandoned idea; its evidence stays). A status changes only on cited evidence, with the owner's approval (skill `review`). Thresholds are written before testing.
